Agent Observatory

Replay how a poisoned complaint row travels through the pipeline and which agents it reaches.

New here? What you are looking at (30 seconds)
1 · The setup

A company keeps public car-safety complaints in a data warehouse. Language-model “agents” read that data: one labels each complaint, others spot trends, answer questions and check data quality.

2 · The attack

An attacker writes a complaint that contains a hidden instruction aimed at the agents (a poisoned row). If an agent obeys it, it may hide a defect or invent a fact.

3 · What to watch

Press Play. Cards are complaint rows moving left to right through the pipeline. Red cards and red figures carry the attacker's text. A shield that shows ✕ means the guard stopped something.

    Compare the three runs: Clean (no attack), Attacked (no defense) and Defended (guard on). Use Split view to see attacked and defended side by side.
